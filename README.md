# 🌑 Shadowgres 🌑

> "Why use Firebase when you can use **FIRE**?" - Shadow Tzu, probably

## What even IS this? 👀

Shadowgres is a BaaS that said "nah" to all that enterprise bloat and decided to be ✨ different ✨. It's basically a database with commitment issues - lightweight, self-hosted, and refuses to touch your wallet.

Store your data. Retrieve your data. Touch grass. In that order.

## ⚡ Features (or whatever)

- **RESTful API** - Because SOAP is for washing hands, not querying databases
- **User-Defined Tables** - Make your own schema. Go wild. We're not your parents.
- **JSON Everything** - Objects? Arrays? Nested chaos? Yes.
- **Self-Hostable** - Your data, your server, your drama
- **No Cap Actually Free** - Unlike *some people* we could mention
